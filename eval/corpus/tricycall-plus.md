# Tricycall Plus

## What Tricycall Plus is
Tricycall Plus is an optional monthly membership for frequent riders. It costs ₱99 per month and renews automatically. You can join from Profile, then Tricycall Plus. It works in every service area.

## Plus benefits
- 10% off Standard rides, including scheduled rides. The discount applies to the base fare and distance charge, not to surge, waiting charges or surcharges. Savings are capped at ₱300 per month.
- 5 minutes of free waiting time at pickup instead of 3.
- Priority matching during surge: your requests are sent to drivers before non-members' requests.

Some benefits belong to a specific feature and are described on that feature's page, such as Scheduled Rides and Lost and Found.

## What Plus does not cover
The Plus discount does not apply to Sabay shared rides or Pasabuy orders. It cannot be combined with a legally mandated discount such as the student or senior citizen discount: if you have both, the app uses whichever is larger for each trip. It can be combined with a promo code.

## Paying for Plus
The membership is charged at the start of each month of membership, from your wallet, GCash, Maya or debit card. Cash is not accepted for Plus. If the payment fails, Plus benefits pause until the payment goes through.

## Cancelling Plus
Cancel any time from Profile, then Tricycall Plus, then Cancel membership. Your benefits continue until the end of the month you already paid for, and the membership does not renew. Plus fees are not refunded for unused days. Deleting your account also cancels Plus.
