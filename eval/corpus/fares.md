# Tricycall Fares

## How fares are calculated
Tricycall fares are based on a minimum fare plus a per-kilometer rate, similar to how tricycle fares work in most Philippine cities. The minimum fare covers the first kilometer of the trip and up to 2 passengers. Every kilometer beyond that is charged at the per-kilometer rate of the service area. Tricycall sets fares per service area, because tricycle fares in the Philippines are regulated locally by each city or municipality rather than by one national rate.

## Fare table for Santa Lucia
These are the Standard ride fares in Santa Lucia, the main service area. San Roque has its own fare table, listed on the Service Areas page.

| Charge | Santa Lucia |
|---|---|
| Minimum fare (first 1 km, up to 2 passengers) | ₱40 |
| Each additional kilometer | ₱15 |
| Each additional passenger beyond 2 | ₱10 flat, regardless of distance |
| Waiting time at pickup, after the free waiting time | ₱2 per minute |

Free waiting time is 3 minutes. Tricycall Plus members get 5 minutes, and pickups at a partner TODA terminal have no waiting charge.

## Fare estimate before booking
The app shows an estimated fare before the passenger confirms a booking. This estimate is based on the shortest available route and current conditions. The final fare may differ slightly from the estimate if the driver takes a different route due to traffic or road closures, or if the trip includes extra waiting time.

## Surge pricing
During periods of high demand or low driver availability (heavy rain, rush hour, major local events), fares may increase temporarily by a multiplier of up to 1.5x. The app always shows the surge multiplier before the passenger confirms the booking, so the passenger can see the adjusted price upfront. Surge applies to the base fare and distance charge only. Surcharges such as the night surcharge are fixed amounts and are never multiplied.

## Payment methods
Passengers can pay Standard rides with cash directly to the driver, or through the in-app wallet using GCash, Maya, or a linked debit card. Drivers cannot refuse a passenger for choosing in-app payment over cash. Scheduled rides and Pasabuy orders can only be paid in-app.

## Discounts
Tricycall honors standard legally mandated discounts, consistent with discount rules applied to tricycles and other public transport in the Philippines:
- Students: 20% off, with valid school ID
- Senior citizens: 20% off, with valid senior citizen ID
- Persons with disabilities (PWD): 20% off, with valid PWD ID
- Solo parents: 10% off, with valid Solo Parent ID

Discounts are applied automatically when the passenger adds a valid ID to their profile. They apply to the base fare and distance charge, not to surge multipliers, waiting-time charges or surcharges. Only one discount applies to a trip, even if a passenger qualifies for more than one; the larger one is used.

## Fare disputes
If a passenger believes they were charged incorrectly, they can open the trip in Trip History and tap "Report a fare issue" within 7 days of the trip. Support reviews the trip's recorded route and timing before issuing a refund or fare adjustment. Cash trips can also be disputed, though refunds for cash trips are issued as in-app wallet credit rather than cash.
