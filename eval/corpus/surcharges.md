# Surcharges

## What surcharges are
A surcharge is a fixed amount added to a fare for a specific condition, such as riding late at night. Unlike surge pricing, a surcharge is not a multiplier: it is the same amount whatever the distance or demand. Surcharges are shown as a separate line in the fare estimate and on the receipt.

## Surcharge table
| Surcharge | Santa Lucia | San Roque | When it applies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night surcharge | ₱10 | ₱8 | Trips that start between 10 PM and 5 AM |
| Large item | ₱10 per item | ₱10 per item | Each large item, such as a sack of rice, a balikbayan box or a bicycle; up to 2 per trip |
| Terminal fee | None | ₱5 | Pickups at the San Roque Public Market terminal |

## How surcharges are added
Surcharges are added after any discount. Legally mandated discounts, the Tricycall Plus discount and promo codes never reduce a surcharge, and surge never multiplies one. For example, a student's fare is discounted first, and the night surcharge is then added in full.

## Large items
Tell the driver about large items when you book, by adding them in the "Extras" step. A Standard tricycle can carry up to 2 large items. Large items are not allowed on Sabay rides.

## Disputing a surcharge
If a surcharge was added that should not have been, for example a night surcharge on a trip that started before 10 PM, report it as a fare issue from Trip History within 7 days.
