# Booking a Ride

## Ride types
Tricycall offers four ways to ride. A Standard ride is the whole tricycle for you and your group. Sabay is a shared ride where you pay for one seat and the driver may pick up other riders going your way. A Scheduled ride is a Standard ride booked ahead of time. Pasabuy is not a ride at all: the driver buys items for you from a store and delivers them. Each has its own page in this help center. This page describes Standard rides unless it says otherwise.

## How to book a ride
1. Open the Home screen and enter your pickup point (or use your current location) and your destination.
2. Choose the ride type. Standard is selected by default.
3. Review the fare estimate. If surge pricing applies, the multiplier is shown here before you confirm. Enter a promo code here if you have one.
4. Choose how you will pay: cash, the in-app wallet, or a linked GCash, Maya, or debit card.
5. Tap "Find a driver."
6. Once a driver accepts, you see their name, photo, plate number, and rating.
7. Follow the driver's live location on the map as they head to your pickup point.
8. Check that the plate number matches before you board.
9. When you arrive, pay (if paying cash) and rate your driver. A receipt is saved in Trip History.

## How driver matching works
Tricycall sends your request to the nearest available driver, usually within about 2 kilometers of your pickup point. The driver has 20 seconds to accept. If they decline or do not respond, the request goes to the next nearest driver automatically. The app keeps searching for up to 3 minutes.

When your pickup pin is within 100 meters of a partner TODA terminal, matching works differently: the request goes to the driver at the front of that terminal's queue instead of the nearest driver. See TODA Terminals.

## If no driver is found
If no driver accepts within 3 minutes, the app shows "No drivers available nearby." You are not charged. You can try again, move your pickup pin to a nearby main road or terminal where drivers are more likely to pass, or try again later. Demand is often higher during rush hour and heavy rain, which can make matching slower.

## Passengers and capacity
A Standard booking is for one whole tricycle, with up to 3 passengers. Standard rides are exclusive, so the driver does not pick up other passengers along the way, unlike per-head rides at tricycle terminals or Sabay rides. The minimum fare covers up to 2 passengers, and each additional passenger costs ₱10 extra.

## Pickup points
You can drag the pin to adjust your pickup point. Tricycles cannot enter some narrow alleys or private roads, so the driver may ask you to meet at the nearest accessible road. You can call or message the driver from the trip screen to coordinate. Calls go through a masked number, so neither of you sees the other's real mobile number.

## Trip receipts
After each trip, a receipt with the route, fare breakdown, and payment method is saved in Trip History. You can reopen it any time to review the charges or report a fare issue.
