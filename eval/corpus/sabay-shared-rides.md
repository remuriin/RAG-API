# Sabay Shared Rides

## What Sabay is
Sabay lets you share a tricycle with other riders heading the same way. You book one seat instead of the whole tricycle, and pay less for it. The driver may stop to pick up or drop off up to 2 other riders along your route. Sabay is available in Santa Lucia and San Roque.

## When you can ride Sabay
Sabay runs from 6 AM to 9 PM every day. It is not offered at night, between 10 PM and 5 AM, or in the hour from 9 PM to 10 PM. Sabay rides cannot be scheduled ahead of time.

## Seat fare
A Sabay seat costs 60% of the Standard fare for your own route, rounded up to the nearest peso, with a minimum seat fare of ₱25. The seat fare is shown before you confirm and does not change if the driver picks up other riders. Surge pricing can apply to Sabay seats during high demand.

Legally mandated discounts (student, senior citizen, PWD, solo parent) apply to the seat fare. The Tricycall Plus discount does not apply to Sabay rides.

## Detours and travel time
The driver may make up to 2 detours to pick up or drop off other riders. Tricycall only matches riders whose detours add no more than 10 minutes to your trip compared with a Standard ride. The app shows the expected arrival time before you confirm.

## What you can bring
Each Sabay booking is for 1 passenger only. Large items, such as a sack of rice, a balikbayan box or a bicycle, are not allowed on Sabay rides, and neither are pets. Book a Standard ride if you are traveling with others, with large items, or with an animal.

## Paying for Sabay
You can pay for a Sabay seat with cash or in-app, the same as a Standard ride. Each rider pays only for their own seat, and pays when they are dropped off, not when the whole trip ends.
