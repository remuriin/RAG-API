# Policy Updates

## About this log
This page lists changes to Tricycall's prices and policies, newest first, with the date each change took effect. It is current as of October 1, 2026. Where another help page has not been updated yet, the change listed here is the one that applies.

## October 2026
**October 1, 2026: Scheduled rides can be booked further ahead.** Scheduled rides can now be booked up to 7 days before pickup, up from 3 days.

## September 2026
**September 30, 2026: TAGULAN25 rainy season promo ended.** The TAGULAN25 code can no longer be used. Rides already completed with it are not affected.

**September 1, 2026: Passenger cancellation fee raised to ₱25.** The fee for cancelling a ride after the free cancellation window, or after the driver has arrived, is now ₱25, up from ₱20. It still goes to the driver in full. The free cancellation windows have not changed.

**September 1, 2026: Tricycall Plus price changed to ₱99 per month.** Plus launched at ₱79 per month. New members pay ₱99 from September 1, and existing members move to ₱99 at their first renewal after that date.

## August 2026
**August 15, 2026: New per-kilometer rate in San Roque.** Following a new fare ordinance from the San Roque local government, each additional kilometer in San Roque now costs ₱13, up from ₱12. The ₱35 minimum fare in San Roque has not changed. Santa Lucia fares have not changed.

## Earlier in 2026
**July 1, 2026: Sabay launched in San Roque.** Sabay shared rides, available in Santa Lucia since April 1, 2026, are now available in San Roque too.

**June 1, 2026: Tricycall Plus launched** at ₱79 per month.

**May 15, 2026: Scheduled rides launched in Santa Lucia**, bookable up to 3 days ahead.

**April 1, 2026: Sabay launched in Santa Lucia.**

## Coming up
**December 1, 2026: Villa Aurora opening (planned).** Tricycall plans to open its third service area, Villa Aurora. Fares for Villa Aurora will be announced before the launch. Until then, rides cannot be booked there.
