# Scheduled Rides

## What a scheduled ride is
A scheduled ride is a Standard ride you book ahead of time, for example for an early trip to the bus terminal or a hospital appointment. You choose the pickup time when you book, and Tricycall finds a driver for you shortly before. Scheduled rides are currently available in Santa Lucia only. They are not yet offered in San Roque.

## How far ahead you can book
You can schedule a ride from 1 hour to 7 days before the pickup time. You can have up to 3 upcoming scheduled rides at a time. Sabay shared rides and Pasabuy orders cannot be scheduled.

## How to schedule a ride
1. On the Home screen, enter your pickup point and destination.
2. Tap the clock icon next to "Find a driver" and choose the date and pickup time.
3. Review the fare and the booking fee, then choose an in-app payment method.
4. Tap "Schedule ride." Your upcoming rides are listed under Trips, then Scheduled.

## Price of a scheduled ride
The fare is fixed when you book: you pay the fare estimate shown at booking, and surge pricing never applies to a scheduled ride, even if demand is high at pickup time. A booking fee of ₱15 is added to each scheduled ride. Tricycall Plus members pay no booking fee. Discounts and the night surcharge apply as they do on any Standard ride. Scheduled rides can only be paid in-app (wallet, GCash, Maya or debit card), not in cash.

## When a driver is assigned
Tricycall starts looking for a driver 30 minutes before your pickup time, and you are notified as soon as one accepts. If no driver has accepted by 10 minutes before pickup, you are notified, the ride is cancelled, and you are not charged anything, including the booking fee.

## Changing or cancelling a scheduled ride
You can change the pickup time or cancel for free up to 1 hour before pickup. If you cancel later than that, the booking fee is kept and nothing else is charged. Once a driver has been assigned and is on the way, the normal passenger cancellation policy applies instead.
