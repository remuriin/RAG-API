# Frequently Asked Questions

## About these answers
These are short answers to the questions riders ask most. Each answer gives the general rule; the full help page for each topic has the details and exceptions, such as differences between service areas and ride types.

## Rides and booking
### How much is the minimum fare?
The minimum fare is ₱40, which covers the first kilometer and up to 2 passengers. After that, each kilometer is ₱15. See Tricycall Fares for the full fare table.

### How long will the driver wait for me?
Waiting is free for the first 3 minutes after the driver arrives. After that, waiting is charged at ₱2 per minute, and after 5 minutes without hearing from you the driver may mark a no-show.

### Can I book a ride in advance?
Yes. With Scheduled rides you can book a tricycle up to 7 days ahead and pick the exact pickup time. A small booking fee applies to each scheduled ride.

### Can I pay with cash?
Yes, you can pay the driver in cash at the end of the trip, or pay in-app with the wallet, GCash, Maya or a debit card. Drivers cannot refuse in-app payment.

## Promos and money
### Is there a promo for rainy days?
Yes! During the rainy season, use the code TAGULAN25 for 25% off Standard rides, up to ₱30 off per ride. Each account can use it 4 times, so save it for the days you need it most.

### Can I get my wallet balance back as cash?
No. Wallet credit can only be used on Tricycall, for rides, Pasabuy and Plus. It does not expire, so you can use it any time.

## Areas
### Is Tricycall available in my city?
Tricycall runs in Santa Lucia and San Roque, and Villa Aurora is planned. Tap "Request my area" in the app to tell us where you would like Tricycall next.

### Can I ride from Santa Lucia to San Roque?
No. A ride must start and end in the same service area, so trips between Santa Lucia and San Roque cannot be booked yet.

## Help
### I left something in the tricycle. What do I do?
Open the trip in Trip History and tap "I lost an item" as soon as possible. The app connects you with the driver to arrange getting it back.
