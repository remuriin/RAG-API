# Pasabuy

## What Pasabuy is
Pasabuy is an errand service: a Tricycall driver buys items for you at a store and brings them to you. You are not riding, so you can stay home. Pasabuy is available in Santa Lucia and San Roque, from 7 AM to 9 PM. The store and your address must be in the same service area.

## How to place a Pasabuy order
1. On the Home screen, tap "Pasabuy."
2. Choose the store, or pin it on the map, and enter your delivery address.
3. List the items and set an item budget.
4. Confirm the delivery fee and pay in-app.

The driver calls you through a masked number if an item is unavailable or costs more than expected.

## Item budget and payment
The driver pays for the items at the store, up to an item budget of ₱1,000 per order. The driver uploads a photo of the store receipt, and you pay the exact receipt amount plus the delivery fee. Pasabuy orders can only be paid in-app (wallet, GCash, Maya or debit card), never in cash.

## Delivery fee
The delivery fee is the Standard fare for the trip from the store to your address, plus a ₱20 Pasabuy fee. Legally mandated discounts and the Tricycall Plus discount do not apply to Pasabuy, because it is not a passenger trip. The night surcharge does not apply either, since Pasabuy closes at 9 PM.

## Items drivers will not buy
Drivers cannot buy or carry:
- alcoholic drinks
- cigarettes, vapes and other tobacco products
- prescription medicines
- live animals
- cash, gift cards or load cards
- items heavier than 10 kg in total, or too large to fit on the passenger seat

## Cancelling a Pasabuy order
You can cancel for free until the driver arrives at the store. After the driver has bought your items, cancelling charges you the receipt amount and the full delivery fee, and the driver still delivers the items to you.
