# Driver Earnings and Tiers

## Service fee
Tricycall keeps a service fee from every trip a driver completes. The driver keeps the rest of the fare, along with all surcharges, waiting charges and the extra-passenger charge. The service fee depends on the driver's tier.

## Driver tiers
| Tier | What it takes (previous Monday to Sunday) | Service fee |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Any verified driver | 15% |
| Silver | At least 40 trips and a rating of 4.6 or higher | 13% |
| Gold | At least 70 trips and a rating of 4.8 or higher | 11% |

Tiers are recalculated every Monday from the previous week's trips and the driver's current rating, and the new service fee applies from that Monday. Each Sabay rider counts as a separate trip. Pasabuy orders count as trips, and the driver keeps the whole ₱20 Pasabuy fee with no service fee taken from it.

## Weekly bonuses
Drivers who complete 50 trips in a Monday to Sunday week earn a ₱300 bonus. Drivers who complete 80 trips earn ₱600 instead. The bonuses do not add up: a driver with 80 trips gets ₱600 in total, not ₱900. Bonuses are paid with the next weekly payout.

## Weekly payouts
Payouts are sent every Monday for the previous Monday to Sunday week, to the GCash or bank account set up in payout settings. The minimum payout is ₱100, and smaller balances roll over to the next week. For cash trips, the driver keeps the fare directly and the service fee is deducted from the next payout. If the deductions are larger than the payout, the remaining amount carries over.

## Earnings dashboard
The earnings dashboard shows each completed trip with its fare, the service fee, surcharges and the driver's take. It also shows progress toward the weekly bonus and the next tier.
