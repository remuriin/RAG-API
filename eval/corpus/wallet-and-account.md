# Wallet and Account

## Creating an account
Sign up with your mobile number and confirm it with a 6-digit one-time code (OTP) sent by SMS. Then enter your name, add an optional email address, and choose whether you are signing up as a passenger or a driver. Drivers continue to the document upload step to get verified.

## Adding an ID for discounts
To get a student, senior citizen, PWD, or solo parent discount, go to Profile, then Discounts, and upload a photo of your valid ID along with the ID number. Student IDs must be valid for the current school year. IDs are reviewed within 1 business day. Once approved, the discount is applied automatically to your eligible fares.

## Linking payment methods
Go to Profile, then Payment methods, and add GCash, Maya, or a debit card. GCash and Maya open a secure screen to authorize the link. Cash is always available as a payment option for Standard and Sabay rides and does not need to be set up. Credit cards are not accepted.

## Using the in-app wallet
You can top up the wallet with GCash, Maya, or a debit card. The minimum top-up is ₱50 and the wallet can hold up to ₱10,000. Wallet credit pays for trips, Pasabuy orders and Tricycall Plus, and also receives refunds, including refunds for cash trips. Wallet credit can only be used on Tricycall. It cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred to another person, and it does not expire.

## Losing access to your number
If you lose access to the mobile number on your account, contact support with a photo of your valid ID so your identity can be confirmed before the number is changed. Changing your number to a new one always requires an OTP sent to the new number.

## Driver earnings
Drivers see what they earned from each trip in the earnings dashboard. Service fees, tiers, bonuses and weekly payouts are explained on the Driver Earnings and Tiers page.

## Deleting your account
Go to Settings, then Delete account. Deletion is processed within 30 days. Please use any remaining wallet credit before deleting, because it cannot be refunded as cash. An active Tricycall Plus membership is cancelled when the account is deleted. Drivers receive any pending payout before their account is closed.
