# TODA Terminals

## What TODA terminals are
A TODA (Tricycle Operators and Drivers' Association) is the local association of tricycle drivers that runs a tricycle terminal, the place where tricycles line up and wait for passengers. Tricycall partners with TODAs in each service area, so you can book a Tricycall ride at their terminals and the drivers waiting there can take Tricycall trips.

## How the terminal queue works
When your pickup pin is within 100 meters of a partner terminal during its opening hours, your request goes to the driver at the front of that terminal's queue, not to the nearest driver. This follows the terminal's first-come, first-served order, which TODAs use for fairness among their drivers. If the first driver does not accept within 20 seconds, the request moves to the next driver in the queue.

## Waiting at a terminal
Pickups at a partner terminal have no waiting charge, because the driver is already waiting in the queue. Pickups at the San Roque Public Market terminal include a ₱5 terminal fee set by the local government; see Surcharges.

## Drivers in the terminal queue
Only drivers who are members of the terminal's TODA can join its queue. A driver adds their TODA membership ID in the app, it is checked within 1 business day, and the driver can then tap "Join queue" when they arrive at the terminal. Drivers who are not TODA members still receive normal requests nearby.

## Lost items at terminals
Items left in a tricycle that cannot be returned directly are kept at the driver's TODA terminal, where you can pick them up during its opening hours. See Lost and Found.
