# Lost and Found

## Reporting a lost item
If you left something in a tricycle, open the trip in Trip History and tap "I lost an item" within 72 hours of the trip. Describe the item and choose how you want it back. The app then connects you with the driver through a masked call, so neither of you sees the other's real number. If you lost your phone, use the "Lost item" form in the help center from any browser and sign in with your mobile number.

## Getting your item back
You can get the item back in two ways:
- The driver brings it to you. This costs a return fee of ₱50, which goes to the driver for the extra trip. Tricycall Plus members pay a ₱25 return fee instead.
- You pick it up for free at the driver's TODA terminal, if the driver is a member of a partner TODA.

The return fee is charged to your in-app payment method once the item is handed over.

## If the driver cannot be reached
If the driver does not answer within 24 hours, Tricycall support takes over and contacts the driver directly. Items handed in by drivers are kept at the driver's TODA terminal, or at the Tricycall office in the service area for drivers who are not TODA members.

## Unclaimed items
Items are kept for 30 days. After that, unclaimed items are donated to a local charity, except IDs and documents, which are handed to the local government office that issued them.

## Responsibility for items
Tricycall and its drivers help return lost items but are not responsible for items left in a tricycle. Tricycall does not pay compensation for items that cannot be found.
