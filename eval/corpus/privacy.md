# Privacy and Your Data

## How Tricycall protects your data
Tricycall processes personal data in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). This page explains what we collect, how long we keep it and what you can do with it. Questions can be sent to our Data Protection Officer at dpo@tricycall.example.

## What we collect
- Your name, mobile number and optional email address
- ID photos and ID numbers you upload for discounts or driver verification
- Trip details: pickup, destination, route and GPS location during trips
- Payment details, kept as tokens from the payment provider; Tricycall never stores full card numbers
- Ratings, comments and support conversations

Tricycall does not record audio or video during trips.

## How long we keep it
- Trip GPS data is kept for 12 months, then anonymized.
- Discount ID photos are deleted 90 days after the ID is approved. The ID number is kept while the discount is active.
- When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted within 30 days, except trip and payment records, which are kept for 5 years because tax rules require it.

## Who we share it with
Your driver sees your first name, rating and pickup point, never your full name or number. Payment providers receive what they need to process payments. Data is shared with the police or a local government office only when the law requires it, or with emergency services when you use the SOS button. Tricycall does not sell personal data or share it with advertisers.

## Your rights
You can download a copy of your data from Settings, then Privacy, then Download my data. The file is ready within 7 days. You can correct your details in Profile at any time, and you can ask the Data Protection Officer to correct or delete data you cannot change yourself.
