# Disputes and Refunds

## What you can dispute
You can ask for a review of a fare, a surcharge, a cancellation fee, a no-show charge, a Pasabuy charge or a lost-item return fee. Open the trip in Trip History and choose the matching option, such as "Report a fare issue." Disputes must be opened within 7 days of the trip. Tricycall Plus fees are not reviewed as disputes; see Tricycall Plus.

## How a review works
Support checks the trip's recorded route, timing and receipts, and may contact the driver. Most reviews are decided within 3 business days. You are notified of the result in the app, and you can follow the status in Trip History. A review can end in a full refund, a partial refund, or no change.

## How refunds are paid
| You paid with | Refund goes to | Time after approval |
|---|---|---|
| Wallet | Wallet | Within 1 business day |
| GCash or Maya | GCash or Maya | 3 to 5 business days |
| Debit card | The same card | 7 to 14 business days |
| Cash | Wallet, as credit | Within 1 business day |

## Asking for a second review
If you disagree with the decision, you can ask for a second review by a senior support agent within 14 days of the first decision, from the same trip in Trip History. The second review is final.

## Safety-related refunds
Refunds connected to a reported safety incident are handled by the safety team, not by general support, and you do not need to open a separate dispute. A ride cancelled with the "safety concern" reason never has a cancellation fee in the first place.

## Card chargebacks
Please open a dispute in the app before asking your bank for a chargeback. If a chargeback is filed while a dispute is still open, the account may be put on hold until the chargeback is resolved.
