# Tricycall Safety

## Emergency SOS button
Every active trip screen, for both passengers and drivers, has an SOS button visible at all times. Tapping it immediately shares the live trip location with Tricycall's safety team and gives the option to call local emergency services directly from within the app. The SOS button does not require confirmation to activate, since hesitating in an actual emergency can cost time.

## Trip sharing
Passengers can share their live trip status, including the driver's name, vehicle details, and real-time location, with a trusted contact through a shareable link. This works even if the trusted contact does not have the Tricycall app installed; the link opens a simplified tracking page in any browser.

## Driver identity verification
Before a trip begins, the passenger can see the driver's name, photo, tricycle plate number, and a driver rating based on past trips. Passengers are encouraged to confirm the plate number matches before boarding, especially at busy terminals where multiple tricycles may be waiting.

## In-trip safety checks
On longer trips, the app may periodically ask the passenger to confirm everything is fine with a simple in-app prompt. If the passenger does not respond and the trip shows signs of an unplanned stop or route deviation, Tricycall's safety team may reach out directly to check in.

## Reporting a safety incident
Passengers and drivers can report a safety incident from the trip history screen, even after the trip has ended. Reports go to a dedicated safety review team, separate from general customer support, and are reviewed within 24 hours. Severe reports (harassment, reckless driving, physical safety threats) are prioritized and may result in immediate suspension of the other party's account pending investigation.

## Driver background checks
All drivers go through a background check as part of onboarding, which includes verifying their driver's license is valid and unexpired, and checking for any disqualifying criminal history. Drivers are required to re-verify their license validity annually.

## Night-time safety features
Between 10 PM and 5 AM, trips are automatically flagged for trip sharing suggestions, and both passenger and driver locations are tracked at a higher frequency for faster response if anything goes wrong.
