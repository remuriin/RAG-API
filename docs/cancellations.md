# Tricycall Cancellations

## Passenger cancellation policy
Passengers can cancel a ride for free within 2 minutes of booking, as long as the driver has not yet started heading to the pickup point. After a driver has been assigned and is en route, cancelling within the next 3 minutes is still free. Cancelling after that 3-minute window, or after the driver has arrived at the pickup point, incurs a cancellation fee of ₱20, which goes to the driver to compensate for their travel time.

If the driver is significantly delayed, more than 10 minutes past the original estimated arrival time, the passenger can cancel without any fee regardless of how much time has passed.

## How to cancel as a passenger
1. Open the active trip screen.
2. Tap "Cancel ride."
3. Select a cancellation reason (changed my mind, driver too far, found another ride, safety concern, other).
4. Confirm the cancellation.

Selecting "safety concern" as the reason skips the cancellation fee automatically and flags the trip for a safety review, regardless of timing.

## Driver cancellation policy
Drivers can cancel an accepted trip, but frequent cancellations affect their driver rating and can trigger a temporary suspension from receiving new trip requests. Drivers are expected to cancel promptly (within 1 minute of accepting) if they realize they cannot complete the trip, rather than leaving the passenger waiting.

If a driver cancels after already being on the way to pickup, the passenger is not charged anything, and is automatically re-matched with another nearby driver when possible.

## No-show policy
If a passenger is not at the pickup location within 5 minutes of the driver's arrival, and does not respond to the driver's call or in-app message, the driver can mark the trip as a no-show. A no-show charges the passenger the full minimum fare, since the driver already completed the trip to the pickup point.

If a driver never arrives and the trip auto-cancels after a prolonged wait, the passenger is not charged and is prompted to request a new ride.

## Repeated cancellations
Passengers who cancel more than 5 rides in a 24-hour period may see a temporary cooldown on booking new rides, to prevent abuse of free cancellations. This cooldown does not apply to cancellations made for a safety concern.
