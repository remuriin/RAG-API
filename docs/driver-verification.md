# Tricycall Driver Verification

## Required documents
To become a verified Tricycall driver, applicants must upload the following during sign-up:
- Valid driver's license (non-professional or professional, both accepted for tricycle operation)
- Official Receipt and Certificate of Registration (OR/CR) for the tricycle
- Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP), issued by the local government unit
- A clear photo of the tricycle, showing the plate number
- A valid government-issued ID matching the name on the driver's license
- A recent selfie photo, for identity matching against the ID

## MTOP requirement
The MTOP is issued by the city or municipality where the tricycle operates and confirms the driver is authorized to operate as a public tricycle in that specific service area. Since tricycle franchising is handled locally rather than nationally, a driver's MTOP is only valid within the LGU that issued it. Drivers who want to operate in a different city or municipality on Tricycall need a separate MTOP from that area.

## Verification timeline
After all documents are submitted, verification typically takes 2 to 5 business days. Applications with unclear document photos or a name mismatch between documents take longer, since they require manual follow-up with the applicant. Drivers can check their status anytime on the verification-pending screen, which shows which documents have been approved and which are still under review.

## Common rejection reasons
- Blurry or partially cropped document photos
- Expired driver's license or MTOP
- Name on government ID does not match the name on the driver's license
- OR/CR does not match the tricycle shown in the vehicle photo
- MTOP issued for a different service area than the one selected during sign-up

Rejected applications can be resubmitted with corrected documents at no cost, and there is no limit on the number of resubmissions.

## Re-verification
Driver's licenses must be re-verified annually to confirm they have not expired. Tricycall sends a reminder notification 30 days before a driver's license verification is due to expire. Driving with an expired license, if not re-verified in time, results in automatic suspension from receiving new trip requests until updated documents are submitted.

## Vehicle requirements
The tricycle must have a valid, current OR/CR and MTOP, and must pass a basic safety check (working lights, horn, and brakes) during onboarding. There is currently no minimum age limit on the tricycle itself, only valid, up-to-date registration and permit documents.
