# Tricycall Service Areas

## Where Tricycall operates
Tricycall is currently running as a pilot in two service areas: Santa Lucia and San Roque. A third area, Villa Aurora, is planned and not yet available. Tricycall is not available anywhere else yet, including big cities such as Metro Manila, Cebu, and Davao.

The area names in this document are fictional and used for testing only.

## Why service areas matter
Tricycle fares and operating permits are regulated by each city or municipality in the Philippines, not nationally. Because of this, Tricycall treats every service area separately, with its own fare table and its own set of verified drivers. A ride must start and end inside the same service area. Tricycall does not take trips that cross out of the area.

## Fares in each area
Santa Lucia is the main test area. Its fares are the ones listed in Tricycall Fares: a ₱40 minimum fare for the first kilometer and up to 2 passengers, then ₱15 per additional kilometer.

San Roque has its own fare table: a ₱35 minimum fare for the first kilometer and up to 2 passengers, then ₱12 per additional kilometer. Everything else works the same in both areas, including discounts, waiting charges, and surge pricing.

## Booking outside a service area
If the pickup point or the destination is outside a service area, the app shows an "outside service area" message and the ride cannot be booked. Moving the pin to a point inside the area fixes this.

## Requesting a new area
Passengers can tap "Request my area" in the app to tell Tricycall where they want the service. A new area only opens once enough drivers there have signed up and been verified with a valid local MTOP.

## Drivers and service areas
A driver can only accept rides in the service area where their MTOP was issued. To work in another area, the driver needs a separate MTOP from that city or municipality, then submits it in the app and changes the service area on their profile. The new document goes through the normal verification, which takes 2 to 5 business days.
