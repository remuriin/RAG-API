# Ratings and Reviews

## Rating a trip
After every completed trip, passengers can rate their driver from 1 to 5 stars, with optional quick tags (for example "safe driving" or "friendly") and a short comment. Ratings can be submitted within 24 hours after the trip ends. Drivers also rate passengers after each trip, using the same 1 to 5 scale.

## How ratings are calculated
A driver's rating is the average of their last 100 rated trips. A passenger's rating works the same way. Individual ratings and comments are anonymous, so the other person only sees the overall average, never who gave which score.

## What low ratings lead to
Drivers whose average falls below 4.2 receive a coaching notice with tips based on the most common feedback. If a driver's average stays below 4.0 after at least 50 rated trips, they may be temporarily paused from receiving new requests while support reviews their record. Passengers with consistently very low ratings may also receive a warning.

## Disputing an unfair rating
Passengers and drivers can flag a rating they think is unfair from Trip History by tapping "Report an issue with this rating" within 7 days of the trip. Support reviews the trip and may remove the rating, for example when it was clearly given in retaliation or because of things outside the service, such as using a discount ID. Cancelling a ride because of a safety concern never counts against a passenger's rating.

## Ratings are not safety reports
A star rating is not a way to report a serious problem such as harassment or reckless driving. Those should be reported as a safety incident from Trip History, which goes to the dedicated safety team and is reviewed within 24 hours. A low rating alone does not start a safety investigation.
